Every expensive tool you are using has a free, open-source alternative. Here are four.

01 AI Coding Agent
Paid
Claude Code
Anthropic's coding agent. Session ends when you close the terminal. No persistent memory. Caps every 5 hours.
$20 – $100 / month
claude.ai/code
Free
Hermes Agent
Runs 24/7. Builds memory across sessions. Learns your workflow. Telegram access. No usage caps. Your model, your cost.
$6 / month hosted — or free locally
myhermes.cloud GitHub
02 AI Design Workspace
Paid
Claude Design
Anthropic's AI design tool. Cloud-only, locked to Opus, tied to a paid subscription. Your data stays on their servers.
Included in Claude Pro/Max
claude.ai/design
Free
Open Design
Runs locally. Uses any CLI agent you already have. 72 design systems, 31 skills, exports to HTML, PDF, PPTX. Apache 2.0.
Free — bring your own model
open-design.ai GitHub
03 Financial Data Terminal
Paid
Bloomberg Terminal
Industry-standard financial data platform. Stocks, derivatives, economics, fixed income. Used by banks and funds globally.
$24,000 / year per seat
bloomberg.com
Free
OpenBB
Open-source financial data platform. Same data categories: stocks, crypto, options, economics, derivatives. Python, CLI, REST API, MCP.
Free — AGPLv3 open source
openbb.co GitHub
04 AI Health Coach
Paid
Whoop AI Coach
Wearable + AI coach that reads your recovery, strain, and sleep. Gives generic recommendations based on your biometric data.
$239 / year + hardware
whoop.com
Free
Custom AI Health Coach
Hermes Agent with health skills. Reads your own data, learns your patterns over time, gives advice based on your actual history. Private.
Free — runs on your own setup
